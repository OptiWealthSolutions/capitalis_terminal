# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Quantitative analysts and portfolio managers at institutional investment firms. Working at a desk, multi-monitor setup, extended sessions under analytical pressure. Primary task on any given screen: interpret risk metrics, run portfolio optimization, monitor macro regime shifts. Expert users — no onboarding hand-holding.

## Product Purpose

Institutional-grade SaaS terminal for portfolio analysis and risk management. Replaces ad-hoc Streamlit tooling with a hardened Next.js + FastAPI platform. Success: an analyst can complete a full portfolio review — screener, optimization, reporting — without leaving the app.

## Brand Personality

Cold. Precise. Uncompromising.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (Notion-style cards, Intercom-style onboarding)
- Bloomberg terminal clutter (information density without hierarchy)
- Consumer fintech (Robinhood-style gradients, neon-on-black crypto aesthetic)
- "Dark mode for dark mode's sake" developer tools with no visual hierarchy

## Design Principles

1. Data over decoration — every visual element earns its place by carrying information.
2. Hierarchy through density — important data is large and immediate; supporting context is small and peripheral.
3. Zero ceremony — no splash screens, no modals, no confirmation dialogs unless irreversible action.
4. Terminal-native — monospace for numbers, proportional for prose, never mixed arbitrarily.
5. Precision over warmth — the interface reflects the discipline of quantitative work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Focus styles always visible. No color-only state communication for critical data.
